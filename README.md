# Hi there, I'm Ahmet 👋

**Mechatronics & Systems Engineer** with 15+ years of hands-on R&D experience taking complex electromechanical systems from initial mechanical concept and PCB layout to deterministic real-time firmware and field validation.

My work sits at the intersection of **robotics, precision motion control, embedded systems, and rapid mechanical prototyping**, spanning defense R&D, humanoid robotics, medical devices, and industrial automation.

---

### 🔧 Core Engineering Domains

- **Robotics & Stabilization:** Upper-body humanoid kinematics, Quasi-Direct Drive (QDD) actuators, multi-axis gimbal stabilization, and sensor fusion.
- **Deterministic Motion Control:** Real-time Linux (PREEMPT_RT) control loops, EtherCAT master implementations (SOEM in CSP mode), and industrial servo drives.
- **Embedded Firmware & Hardware:** Bare-metal & RTOS firmware development, high-speed deterministic communication bridges (CAN-FD, RS485, SPI/SSI), and multi-layer schematic/PCB design.
- **Mechanical Design & Fabrication:** Structural and vibration-isolated mechanical design, CAD/CAM workflows, and in-house CNC machining.
